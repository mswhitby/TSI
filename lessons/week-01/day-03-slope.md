## Mini-lesson — 7 minutes



Slope m = change in y ÷ change in x = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). Use the same point order in the numerator and denominator.

In y = mx + b, m is the rate and b is the value of y at x = 0. A positive slope increases; a negative slope decreases.

A horizontal line has slope 0. A vertical line has no defined slope because its change in x is zero; division by zero is undefined.



### Worked examples



**Find slope through (1, 5) and (4, 14).**



1. Change in y: 14 − 5 = 9. Change in x: 4 − 1 = 3.

2. Slope = 9 ÷ 3 = 3.

3. Use (1, 5): 5 = 3(1) + b, so b = 2 and y = 3x + 2.



**A rental costs C = 4h + 12 dollars for h hours. Interpret the model and find the cost for 3 hours.**



1. The rate is $4 per hour. The initial charge is $12.

2. For h = 3, C = 4(3) + 12 = $24.

3. The initial charge is not the hourly rate.
